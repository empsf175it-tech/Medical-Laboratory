Privacy & Patient Data Policy

Vitalis Labs Clinical Information Compliance
1. Scope and Healthcare Data Protection

At Vitalis Labs, safeguarding Protected Health Information (PHI) and biological specimen integrity is our highest operational priority. Diagnostic records, billing details, and clinical data are protected under high-grade 256-bit encryption.

2. Information Collection

We collect patient demographic information, test requisition parameters, ordering physician records, and specimen collection timestamps solely for accurate diagnostic reporting and mandatory health accreditation records.

3. Digital Report Security

Electronic reports are accessible only via verified two-factor matching (Patient ID + Report Reference ID) through our encrypted Patient Portal. We never share patient records with third-party advertisers.

4. Specimen Storage & Disposal

Biological samples are safely discarded after clinical retention periods in full accordance with biomedical waste management standards.

Terms & Service Conditions

Laboratory Testing & Home Collection Guidelines
1. Diagnostic Testing Agreement

By booking a laboratory test or health package at Vitalis Labs, you authorize our certified phlebotomists and technologists to collect biological specimens and process the ordered diagnostic profiles.

2. Fasting & Preparation Responsibility

Patients are responsible for following fasting requirements (e.g. 10–12 hours for Lipid Profile / Glucose tests) to ensure assay accuracy. Non-compliance may result in invalid baseline figures requiring re-collection.

3. Report Turnaround Times (TAT)

Routine blood assays are typically finalized within 6 to 12 hours. Specialized endocrine, microbiological cultures, and molecular PCR assays may require 24 to 48 hours for clinical incubation.

4. Medical Interpretation Disclaimer

Laboratory values are clinical diagnostic indicators and must be interpreted by a licensed physician in context with overall clinical symptoms and medical history.
